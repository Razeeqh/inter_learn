## THE 60-SECOND VERSION

The s-block is just **the two columns on the far left of the periodic table**.

```
  GROUP 1 (ns1)  ->  loses 1 electron  ->  forms M+   ->  MOST reactive metals
  GROUP 2 (ns2)  ->  loses 2 electrons ->  forms M2+  ->  slightly less reactive
```

Almost every fact in this chapter comes from **one root cause: SIZE**.

```
  GOING DOWN a group, the atom gets BIGGER:

      outer electron sits FARTHER from the nucleus
              |
              v
      it is held LOOSELY  ->  IONISATION ENTHALPY goes DOWN
              |
              v
      the metal gives up its electron more easily  ->  MORE REACTIVE
              |
              v
      the ion formed is BIGGER  ->  it pulls water molecules LESS
              |
              v
      HYDRATION ENTHALPY goes DOWN  and  LATTICE ENERGY goes DOWN


  GOING ACROSS from Group 1 to Group 2 (same period):

      more protons + smaller size  ->  IE UP, harder, higher m.p.,
      denser, ion is smaller so HYDRATION ENTHALPY is MUCH bigger,
      metal is LESS reactive.
```

**And two elements refuse to obey the rules — they are too small:**

```
      Li  (top of Group 1)   ---diagonal--->   Mg  (Group 2)
      Be  (top of Group 2)   ---diagonal--->   Al  (Group 13)
```

**If you understand only that, you can reason out half the answers in the paper.**

## THE OXYGEN RULE (one small box, several marks)

```
  When an alkali metal is burnt in excess air:

       Li          ->  Li2O                 OXIDE      (has  O2-   )
       Na          ->  Na2O2                PEROXIDE   (has  O2 2- )
       K, Rb, Cs   ->  KO2, RbO2, CsO2      SUPEROXIDE (has  O2 -  )

  WHY?   A BIG cation stabilises a BIG anion.
         Li+ is tiny, so it can only hold the small O2- ion.
         K+, Rb+, Cs+ are large, so they can hold the large O2- ion.

  MEMORY LINE:   "Little Oxide , Sodium Peroxide , King Super"
                    Li2O           Na2O2            KO2

  Group 2 forms only NORMAL OXIDES (MO) on burning.
  (BaO2 and SrO2 peroxides exist but are made separately, not by burning.)
```

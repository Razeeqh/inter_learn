# =====================================================
# PATTERN 1 — NAME THE MONOMER OF A GIVEN POLYMER
# =====================================================

The single most common polymers question in every exam.

### Q1. The monomer of Teflon is
(a) tetrafluoroethene (b) tetrachloroethene (c) trifluoroethene (d) vinyl fluoride

**ANSWER: (a)**
```
  Teflon = polytetrafluoroethene, PTFE.
        n CF2 = CF2  ->  -[- CF2 - CF2 -]-n
```

---

### Q2. The monomers of nylon 6,6 are
(a) caprolactam only (b) adipic acid and hexamethylenediamine
(c) ethylene glycol and terephthalic acid (d) phenol and formaldehyde

**ANSWER: (b)**
```
  Adipic acid       HOOC-(CH2)4-COOH        (6 carbons)
  Hexamethylene-    H2N-(CH2)6-NH2          (6 carbons)
  diamine                                    hence "6,6"

  (c) would be terylene, (d) would be Bakelite.
```

---

### Q3. Nylon 6 is obtained from
(a) adipic acid (b) caprolactam (c) glycine (d) amino caproic acid

**ANSWER: (b)**
```
  Caprolactam, a cyclic amide, heated with water at 533-543 K.
  ONE monomer only. That is the difference from nylon 6,6.
```

---

### Q4. The monomer of natural rubber is
(a) 1,3-butadiene (b) chloroprene (c) isoprene (d) styrene

**ANSWER: (c)**
```
  Isoprene = 2-methyl-1,3-butadiene.
  Natural rubber = cis-1,4-polyisoprene.
```

---

### Q5. Neoprene is obtained by the polymerisation of
(a) isoprene (b) chloroprene (c) vinyl chloride (d) butadiene + styrene

**ANSWER: (b)**
```
  Chloroprene = 2-chloro-1,3-butadiene.
  Careful: (c) vinyl chloride gives PVC, NOT neoprene.
```

---

### Q6. Buna-N is a copolymer of
(a) butadiene and styrene (b) butadiene and acrylonitrile
(c) isoprene and styrene (d) chloroprene and acrylonitrile

**ANSWER: (b)**

> **SHORTCUT:** In "Buna", **Bu** = butadiene, **na** = natrium (sodium).
> The last letter is the partner: **S** = **S**tyrene, **N** = acrylo**N**itrile.
> That decoder answers every Buna question ever set.

---

### Q7. Orlon (Acrilan) is made from
(a) acrylonitrile (b) acrylic acid (c) styrene (d) vinyl acetate

**ANSWER: (a)**
```
  Polyacrylonitrile,  n CH2=CH-CN  ->  -[-CH2-CH(CN)-]-n
  Used as a substitute for WOOL.
```

---

### Q8. Terylene is a condensation polymer of ethylene glycol and
(a) phthalic acid (b) benzoic acid (c) terephthalic acid (d) adipic acid

**ANSWER: (c)**
```
  ethylene glycol + TEREphthalic acid  ->  TERylene    (memory hook)
  ethylene glycol + PHTHALIC acid      ->  GLYPTAL
```

---

### Q9. Bakelite is obtained from phenol and
(a) acetaldehyde (b) formaldehyde (c) benzaldehyde (d) acetone

**ANSWER: (b)**

---

### Q10. The polymer used for unbreakable crockery is made from
(a) urea and formaldehyde (b) melamine and formaldehyde
(c) phenol and formaldehyde (d) glycerol and phthalic acid

**ANSWER: (b)**
```
  MELAMINE-formaldehyde  ->  unbreakable crockery
  UREA-formaldehyde      ->  laminated sheets, adhesives
  PHENOL-formaldehyde    ->  Bakelite, electrical switches
  glycerol/glycol + phthalic acid -> glyptal, paints
```

# =====================================================
# PATTERN 2 — ADDITION OR CONDENSATION?
# =====================================================

> **SHORTCUT:** Look at the monomer.
> **C=C present -> ADDITION.  -OH / -COOH / -NH2 present -> CONDENSATION.**

### Q11. Which of the following is a condensation polymer?
(a) polystyrene (b) Teflon (c) nylon 6,6 (d) neoprene

**ANSWER: (c)**
```
  (a), (b), (d) all come from C=C monomers -> addition.
  Nylon 6,6 loses water -> condensation.
```

---

### Q12. Which of the following is an addition polymer?
(a) terylene (b) Bakelite (c) PVC (d) glyptal

**ANSWER: (c)**

---

### Q13. In which polymerisation is a small molecule eliminated?
(a) addition (b) condensation (c) both (d) neither

**ANSWER: (b)**

---

### Q14. The molecular mass of an addition polymer is
(a) always less than n x monomer mass (b) an exact multiple of the monomer mass
(c) always greater than n x monomer mass (d) unrelated to the monomer mass

**ANSWER: (b)**
```
  Nothing is eliminated in addition polymerisation, so the polymer
  weighs exactly what all the monomers weighed.
  (In CONDENSATION the answer would be (a), because water leaves.)
```

---

### Q15. Which pair is correctly matched?
(a) PVC — condensation (b) nylon 6 — addition
(c) Buna-S — addition (d) Bakelite — addition

**ANSWER: (c)**
```
  Buna-S comes from butadiene and styrene, both with C=C. It is an
  addition COPOLYMER. The other three are all mismatched.
```

---

# =====================================================
# PATTERN 3 — HOMOPOLYMER OR COPOLYMER?
# =====================================================

### Q16. Which of the following is a copolymer?
(a) Teflon (b) neoprene (c) Buna-S (d) polystyrene

**ANSWER: (c)**

> **SHORTCUT:** Count the monomers. **One monomer = homopolymer.**
> Neoprene is a trap — it comes from chloroprene ALONE, so it is a
> **homopolymer**, even though it is a synthetic rubber like Buna-S.

---

### Q17. Which of the following is NOT a copolymer?
(a) nylon 6,6 (b) terylene (c) Buna-N (d) nylon 6

**ANSWER: (d)**
```
  Nylon 6 comes from caprolactam only -> homopolymer.
  Nylon 6,6, terylene and Buna-N each need TWO monomers.
```

---

### Q18. Copolymerisation means
(a) polymerisation of one monomer at two temperatures
(b) polymerisation of a mixture of more than one kind of monomer
(c) polymerisation followed by cross-linking
(d) polymerisation in the presence of a catalyst

**ANSWER: (b)**

---

# =====================================================
# PATTERN 4 — CLASSIFICATION BY MOLECULAR FORCES
# =====================================================

### Q19. The polymer with the WEAKEST intermolecular forces is
(a) nylon 6,6 (b) Buna-S (c) terylene (d) Bakelite

**ANSWER: (b)**
```
  Elastomers have the weakest forces. Buna-S is an elastomer.
```

---

### Q20. The strongest intermolecular forces are found in
(a) elastomers (b) thermoplastics (c) fibres (d) elastomers and thermoplastics equally

**ANSWER: (c)**
```
  FIBRES - hydrogen bonding and dipole-dipole attraction.
```

---

### Q21. Which of the following is a fibre?
(a) polythene (b) Buna-N (c) Bakelite (d) terylene

**ANSWER: (d)**

---

### Q22. Which of the following is a thermosetting polymer?
(a) polythene (b) PVC (c) Bakelite (d) polystyrene

**ANSWER: (c)**

> **SHORTCUT for the exam hall:** anything ending in
> "**-formaldehyde resin**" and **Bakelite** are thermosetting.
> Everything that starts with "**poly**-" (polythene, polystyrene,
> polypropene, PVC) is thermoplastic.

---

### Q23. Thermosetting polymers
(a) soften on heating and can be remoulded
(b) set permanently on heating and cannot be remoulded
(c) melt at low temperature
(d) are always linear

**ANSWER: (b)**

---

### Q24. Which of the following can be recycled by melting?
(a) Bakelite (b) melamine-formaldehyde (c) polythene (d) urea-formaldehyde

**ANSWER: (c)**
```
  Only THERMOPLASTICS can be melted and remoulded. The other three are
  cross-linked thermosetting resins - they char instead of melting.
```

---

### Q25. Elastomers are elastic because
(a) their chains are cross-linked heavily
(b) their chains are randomly coiled with a few cross-links and weak forces
(c) their chains are close packed and crystalline
(d) they have hydrogen bonds between chains

**ANSWER: (b)**

# =====================================================
# PATTERN 5 — CLASSIFICATION BY STRUCTURE
# =====================================================

### Q26. Low density polythene is
(a) linear (b) branched (c) cross-linked (d) crystalline

**ANSWER: (b)**
```
  LDPE  ->  BRANCHED  ->  loose packing  ->  LOW density, flexible
  HDPE  ->  LINEAR    ->  close packing  ->  HIGH density, tough

  Same monomer (ethene), different catalyst, different structure.
```

---

### Q27. HDPE is prepared using
(a) benzoyl peroxide at 1000 atm (b) Ziegler-Natta catalyst at 6-7 atm
(c) sodium metal (d) zinc acetate and antimony trioxide

**ANSWER: (b)**
```
  Ziegler-Natta = triethylaluminium + titanium tetrachloride.
  (a) gives LDPE, (c) gives Buna-S, (d) gives terylene.
```

---

### Q28. Which of the following is a cross-linked polymer?
(a) HDPE (b) nylon 6,6 (c) melamine-formaldehyde (d) polypropene

**ANSWER: (c)**

---

### Q29. Which natural polymer has a branched structure?
(a) cellulose (b) amylose (c) amylopectin (d) nylon 6

**ANSWER: (c)**
```
  Amylopectin is the BRANCHED component of starch.
  Glycogen is also branched. Cellulose is linear.
```

# PATTERN 8 — RUBBER AND VULCANISATION
# =====================================================

### Q37. Natural rubber is
(a) trans-1,4-polyisoprene (b) cis-1,4-polyisoprene
(c) cis-1,2-polybutadiene (d) trans-polychloroprene

**ANSWER: (b)**
```
  CIS  -> natural rubber, elastic
  TRANS -> gutta percha, hard and non-elastic
```

---

### Q38. Vulcanisation of rubber is carried out by heating it with
(a) phosphorus (b) sulphur (c) carbon black (d) silica

**ANSWER: (b)**

---

### Q39. The temperature range used for vulcanisation is
(a) 273-300 K (b) 373-415 K (c) 500-600 K (d) 1000 K

**ANSWER: (b)**

---

### Q40. The percentage of sulphur used to make tyre rubber is about
(a) 0.5% (b) 5% (c) 30% (d) 60%

**ANSWER: (b)**
```
   5 %  sulphur  ->  TYRE rubber   (still flexible)
  30 %  sulphur  ->  BATTERY CASE  (hard, ebonite)
```

---

### Q41. Vulcanisation makes rubber
(a) softer and stickier (b) soluble in petrol
(c) stronger, with a wider useful temperature range (d) biodegradable

**ANSWER: (c)**

---

### Q42. Which synthetic rubber has the best resistance to petrol and
lubricating oils?
(a) Buna-S (b) Buna-N (c) natural rubber (d) polystyrene

**ANSWER: (b)**
```
  Buna-N  -> oil seals, tank lining (petrol / lubricating oils)
  Neoprene-> vegetable and mineral oils
  Buna-S  -> abrasion resistance, tyres
```

---

### Q43. Raw natural rubber becomes soft and sticky above
(a) 283 K (b) 300 K (c) 335 K (d) 415 K

**ANSWER: (c)**
```
  Useful range of raw rubber:  283 K  to  335 K
  below 283 K -> hard and brittle
  above 335 K -> soft and sticky
```

# PATTERN 11 — MOLECULAR MASS AND PDI
# =====================================================

### Q54. The polydispersity index of a natural polymer is
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) between 1 and 2 (d) greater than 2

**ANSWER: (b)**
```
  Natural polymers are MONODISPERSE - all chains the same length,
  so Mw = Mn and PDI = 1.
```

---

### Q55. For any polymer sample
(a) Mw < Mn (b) Mw = Mn always (c) Mw >= Mn (d) no relation exists

**ANSWER: (c)**

---

### Q56. A sample contains 100 molecules of mass 10,000 u and 200 molecules
of mass 40,000 u. The number average molecular mass is
(a) 25,000 (b) 30,000 (c) 36,667 (d) 40,000

**ANSWER: (b)**
```
          (100 x 10000) + (200 x 40000)     1000000 + 8000000
   Mn = ---------------------------------- = -------------------
                   100 + 200                       300

                 9 000 000
             = -------------  =  30 000
                    300

  (36,667 is the WEIGHT average - a deliberate distractor.)
```

---

### Q57. The degree of polymerisation of a polythene sample of molecular
mass 56,000 u is
(a) 1000 (b) 2000 (c) 2800 (d) 4000

**ANSWER: (b)**
```
  Monomer = ethene = 28 u

        56000
   n = -------  =  2000
          28
```

> **SHORTCUT:** memorise the monomer masses.
> ethene 28, propene 42, vinyl chloride 62.5, styrene 104,
> tetrafluoroethene 100, acrylonitrile 53, isoprene 68.

---

### Q58. If Mn = 40,000 and Mw = 50,000, the PDI is
(a) 0.8 (b) 1.0 (c) 1.25 (d) 10,000

**ANSWER: (c)**
```
   PDI = Mw / Mn = 50000 / 40000 = 1.25
```

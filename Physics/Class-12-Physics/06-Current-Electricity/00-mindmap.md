## THE 60-SECOND VERSION

Think of electricity as **water flowing in a pipe**. That one picture explains
the whole chapter:

```
  WATER PIPE                     ELECTRIC CIRCUIT
  --------------------------     ---------------------------------
  Water flowing per second   ->  CURRENT   I      (ampere)
  Pump / height difference   ->  VOLTAGE   V      (volt)
  Narrow, rough pipe         ->  RESISTANCE R     (ohm)
  Pump's full strength       ->  EMF       E
  Pump's own friction        ->  INTERNAL RESISTANCE r
  Water wasted as heat       ->  POWER     P = V I
```

More push (V) gives more flow (I). More resistance (R) gives less flow.
That single sentence is **Ohm's law**, and it runs the entire chapter.

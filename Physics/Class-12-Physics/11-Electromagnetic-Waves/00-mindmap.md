## THE 60-SECOND VERSION

```
  1.  Ampere's law said:  magnetic field is made by CURRENT.
      But in a charging capacitor there is a magnetic field in the GAP
      where NO current flows.  So Ampere's law is INCOMPLETE.

  2.  Maxwell fixed it: a CHANGING ELECTRIC FIELD also makes a magnetic
      field. He called this the DISPLACEMENT CURRENT.

  3.  Now look at the pair:
          a changing MAGNETIC field  makes an  ELECTRIC field  (Faraday)
          a changing ELECTRIC field  makes a   MAGNETIC field  (Maxwell)

      Each one keeps producing the other. They march off through empty
      space together. THAT is an electromagnetic wave.

  4.  The maths gave the speed:   c = 1 / sqrt(mu0 eps0) = 3 x 10^8 m/s
      which is exactly the measured speed of LIGHT.
      So light IS an electromagnetic wave.

  5.  Change the frequency and you get the same thing wearing different
      clothes: radio, microwave, infrared, light, UV, X-ray, gamma.
```

That is the entire chapter. Everything else is detail hanging off those five lines.

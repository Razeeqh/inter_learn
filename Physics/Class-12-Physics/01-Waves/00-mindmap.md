## THE 60-SECOND VERSION

A wave is a **disturbance that travels and carries ENERGY, while the particles
of the medium only vibrate about their own fixed places**. Nothing is
transported except energy and momentum.

```
  1.  A wave moves.  Its particles do NOT move along with it.

  2.  ONE equation rules the whole chapter:      v  =  f  x  lambda

  3.  The SPEED depends only on the MEDIUM.
      The FREQUENCY depends only on the SOURCE.
      So when a wave enters a new medium:
          f stays the SAME,  v and lambda CHANGE.

  4.  Two waves meeting simply ADD (superposition).
          Travelling in the SAME direction   ->  INTERFERENCE
          Travelling in OPPOSITE directions  ->  STATIONARY WAVES
          Slightly different frequencies     ->  BEATS

  5.  If the source or the listener MOVES, the pitch changes -> DOPPLER EFFECT.
```

That is the entire chapter. Everything else is detail hanging off those five lines.

**Three numbers you must be able to quote instantly:**

```
  Speed of sound in air at 0 deg C   =  332 m/s
  Speed of sound in air at 20 deg C  =  343 m/s
  Speed of light / EM waves          =  3 x 10^8 m/s
  Rise in speed of sound per deg C   =  0.61 m/s
```

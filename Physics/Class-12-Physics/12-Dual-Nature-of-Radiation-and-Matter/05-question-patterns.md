# Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter — Every Question Pattern That Can Be Asked

A "pattern" is a question SHAPE. The numbers change every year. The shape does
not.

For each one, learn:
- **TRIGGER** — how to recognise it in one glance
- **METHOD** — the fixed steps
- **TRAP** — where marks get lost

---

```
  PATTERN 1 — DEFINE A TERM                                       2 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Define work function / threshold frequency / threshold
            wavelength / stopping potential / saturation current /
            photoelectric effect."
  METHOD    Give the DEFINITION in one clean sentence, then add the
            defining FORMULA underneath.
              work function      -> minimum energy to just free an
                                    electron from the surface. W0 = h nu0
              threshold freq.    -> minimum frequency below which no
                                    emission occurs. nu0 = W0/h
              threshold lambda   -> MAXIMUM wavelength that still causes
                                    emission. lambda0 = c/nu0
              stopping potential -> negative anode potential at which the
                                    photocurrent becomes zero. eV0 = Kmax
              saturation current -> maximum current, when ALL emitted
                                    electrons are collected.
  TRAP      Writing only the formula. The examiner wants the SENTENCE
            first. Also: frequency has a MINIMUM, wavelength has a
            MAXIMUM.
```

```
  PATTERN 2 — THE FOUR TYPES OF ELECTRON EMISSION                 2-4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Name the types of electron emission" / "What is thermionic
            emission?"
  METHOD    Name them with the ENERGY SOURCE, and one example each:
              1 THERMIONIC   - heat          - CRT / X-ray filament
              2 FIELD (cold) - strong E field (~10^8 V/m) - field
                               emission microscope
              3 PHOTOELECTRIC- light         - photocell
              4 SECONDARY    - fast electrons hitting the surface
                             - photomultiplier
  TRAP      Forgetting SECONDARY emission -- it is the one students drop.
            Memory line: HEAT, FIELD, LIGHT, HIT.
```

```
  PATTERN 3 — DESCRIBE THE PHOTOELECTRIC EXPERIMENT               4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Describe the experimental arrangement used to study the
            photoelectric effect."
  METHOD    1. Draw the labelled diagram: evacuated tube, quartz window,
               emitter C, collector A, micro-ammeter, voltmeter,
               potentiometer, commutator.
            2. State WHY each part is there:
                 evacuated  -> electrons not stopped by air
                 quartz     -> transmits ultraviolet light
                 commutator -> makes A positive or negative, so V0 can
                               be measured
            3. State that frequency, intensity and potential can be
               varied INDEPENDENTLY.
  TRAP      Drawing the circuit but not labelling the plates C and A,
            or omitting the word EVACUATED.
```

```
  PATTERN 4 — STATE THE LAWS / EXPERIMENTAL RESULTS               4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "State the laws of photoelectric emission" / "What are the
            experimental observations of the photoelectric effect?"
  METHOD    Give FOUR (or five) numbered results, each with its graph:
            1. Saturation current is PROPORTIONAL to intensity.
            2. Stopping potential is INDEPENDENT of intensity.
            3. Kmax and V0 increase LINEARLY with frequency.
            4. Below nu0 there is NO emission, however intense.
            5. Emission is INSTANTANEOUS ( < 10^-9 s ).
  TRAP      Answering in words only. Every graph you draw is worth marks
            -- draw all of them, with both axes labelled.
```

```
  PATTERN 5 — DRAW AND EXPLAIN THE CURRENT-vs-POTENTIAL GRAPH     4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Explain the effect of potential on the photoelectric
            current" / "Define saturation current and stopping
            potential using a graph."
  METHOD    1. Draw three curves for three intensities at ONE frequency.
            2. Mark the SATURATION level (different for each curve).
            3. Mark -V0 on the negative axis (the SAME for all three).
            4. Explain the four regions: zero current at large negative
               V; zero exactly at -V0; small current at V = 0;
               saturation at large positive V.
            5. Conclude: intensity -> current ; frequency -> V0.
  TRAP      Drawing the three curves meeting the axis at DIFFERENT
            points. They must all meet at the same -V0 because the
            frequency is the same.
```

```
  PATTERN 6 — DRAW AND EXPLAIN THE V0-vs-FREQUENCY GRAPH          4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Explain the effect of frequency on the stopping potential"
            / "How is Planck's constant determined?"
  METHOD    1. Start from  e V0 = h nu - W0 , so V0 = (h/e)nu - W0/e.
            2. Draw the straight line. Mark nu0 on the nu-axis and
               -W0/e on the V0-axis.
            3. State SLOPE = h/e = 4.14 x 10^-15 V s.
            4. Hence h = e x slope  -> this is how h is measured.
            5. Add that the lines for different metals are PARALLEL.
  TRAP      Saying the slope is h. It is h/e when the y-axis is V0.
            The slope is h only when the y-axis is Kmax.
```

```
  PATTERN 7 — WHY THE WAVE THEORY FAILS                           4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Explain the failure of the wave theory" / "Why is the
            classical theory unable to explain the photoelectric
            effect?"
  METHOD    Four paired statements. Always write them as a pair:
            "WAVE THEORY PREDICTS ... BUT EXPERIMENT SHOWS ..."
              1. no threshold should exist  BUT there IS a nu0
              2. Kmax should rise with intensity  BUT it does not
              3. Kmax should not depend on frequency BUT it does,
                 linearly
              4. a long time lag is expected BUT emission is instant
  TRAP      Writing only what the experiment shows without saying what
            the wave theory predicted. Half the marks are in the
            contrast.
```

```
  PATTERN 8 — STATE AND DERIVE EINSTEIN'S EQUATION                4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "State and explain Einstein's photoelectric equation" /
            "Derive the photoelectric equation."
  METHOD    1. State the PHOTON POSTULATE: light travels as packets of
               energy h nu, and ONE photon is absorbed COMPLETELY by
               ONE electron.
            2. Energy h nu is used partly as W0 to free the electron.
            3. The rest becomes kinetic energy.
            4. A SURFACE electron loses only W0, so it has the MAXIMUM
               kinetic energy.
            5. Conservation of energy:  h nu = W0 + Kmax .
            6. Write the alternate forms: Kmax = h(nu - nu0) = e V0.
  TRAP      Forgetting to say "one photon, one electron". That sentence
            is the whole reason a threshold frequency exists.
```

```
  PATTERN 9 — USE EINSTEIN'S EQUATION TO EXPLAIN THE RESULTS      4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "How does Einstein's equation explain the experimental
            observations?"
  METHOD    Take the four results one at a time:
              threshold  -> if h nu < W0 the packet is too small, and
                            packets cannot be added
              current    -> intensity = photons per second, each frees
                            at most one electron
              Kmax vs    -> intensity changes the NUMBER of photons,
              intensity     not the energy of one
              Kmax vs    -> Kmax = h nu - W0 is a straight line
              frequency
              instant    -> a single collision, nothing to accumulate
  TRAP      Repeating the observations instead of EXPLAINING them.
            Every sentence must begin from h nu = W0 + Kmax.
```

```
  PATTERN 10 — PROPERTIES OF A PHOTON                             2-4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Write the properties of a photon" / "Is a photon deflected
            by a magnetic field?"
  METHOD    E = h nu = hc/lambda ; p = h/lambda = E/c ;
            REST MASS = 0 ; CHARGE = 0 ; hence NOT deflected by
            electric or magnetic fields ; always travels at c ;
            energy and momentum are conserved in collisions but the
            NUMBER of photons is not.
  TRAP      Saying "a photon has no mass so it has no momentum".
            Wrong -- p = h/lambda is non-zero.
```

```
  PATTERN 11 — FIND THE ENERGY / MOMENTUM OF A PHOTON             2 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Find the energy of a photon of wavelength ... " /
            "Find its momentum."
  METHOD    1. E(eV) = 12400 / lambda(A).
            2. To get joules, multiply by 1.6 x 10^-19.
            3. p = h / lambda  (or p = E/c if E is already in joules).
  TRAP      Using 12400 with the wavelength in metres or nanometres.
            The wavelength MUST be in angstrom.
```

```
  PATTERN 12 — HOW MANY PHOTONS PER SECOND                        2-4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "A 100 W lamp emits light of wavelength ... How many
            photons per second?"
  METHOD    1. Energy of one photon: E = 12400/lambda(A) eV, then
               convert to joules.
            2. n = P / E .
            3. If an intensity I and an area A are given instead,
               use n = I A / E.
  TRAP      Forgetting to convert eV to joules before dividing the
            power in watts. The answer should come out around 10^18
            to 10^21.
```

```
  PATTERN 13 — FIND W0 , nu0 OR lambda0 FROM ONE ANOTHER          2 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "The threshold wavelength is 5000 A. Find the work
            function." (or any permutation of the three)
  METHOD    W0(eV) = 12400 / lambda0(A)
            lambda0(A) = 12400 / W0(eV)
            nu0 = W0 / h = c / lambda0
  TRAP      Mixing units: if W0 is given in JOULES use nu0 = W0/h;
            if in eV use the 12400 shortcut. Do not mix the two.
```

```
  PATTERN 14 — WILL EMISSION OCCUR?                               2 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Light of 6000 A falls on a metal of work function 2.5 eV.
            Will photoelectrons be emitted?"
  METHOD    Two equivalent tests -- use whichever is quicker:
              ENERGY TEST      : is  12400/lambda  >  W0(eV) ?
              WAVELENGTH TEST  : is  lambda  <  lambda0 ?
            Answer YES or NO and give the comparison that proves it.
  TRAP      Comparing wavelengths the wrong way round. A LONGER
            wavelength means LOWER energy. lambda must be SMALLER
            than lambda0 for emission.
```

```
  PATTERN 15 — FIND Kmax AND THE STOPPING POTENTIAL               2-4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Light of wavelength ... falls on a metal of work function
            ... Find the maximum kinetic energy and the stopping
            potential."
  METHOD    1. E(eV) = 12400 / lambda(A)
            2. Kmax = E - W0        (in eV)
            3. V0 = Kmax numerically, in volts. NO calculation needed.
            4. If joules are wanted, multiply Kmax by 1.6 x 10^-19.
  TRAP      Writing V0 in eV. The stopping potential is a POTENTIAL --
            the unit is the VOLT.
```

```
  PATTERN 16 — FIND THE MAXIMUM VELOCITY OF THE PHOTOELECTRONS    4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Find the maximum velocity / speed of the emitted
            electrons."
  METHOD    1. Get Kmax in eV as in Pattern 15.
            2. Convert to joules:  Kmax x 1.6 x 10^-19 .
            3. v = sqrt( 2 Kmax / m ) with m = 9.1 x 10^-31 kg.
            SHORTCUT: v = 5.93 x 10^5 x sqrt( Kmax in eV )  m/s.
  TRAP      Substituting Kmax in eV directly into (1/2)mv^2. It must be
            in JOULES. The answer should land between 10^5 and 10^6 m/s.
```

```
  PATTERN 17 — INCIDENT FREQUENCY GIVEN AS A MULTIPLE OF nu0      2 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Light of frequency 2 nu0 (or 3 nu0 , 1.5 nu0) falls on a
            metal of threshold frequency nu0. Find Kmax."
  METHOD    Kmax = h(nu - nu0) = h(n nu0 - nu0) = (n - 1) h nu0
                 = (n - 1) W0
            So at 2nu0 -> Kmax = W0 ; at 3nu0 -> 2W0 ; at 1.5nu0 -> W0/2.
  TRAP      Answering "n W0". The ratio uses (nu - nu0), never nu alone.
            Ratios between two such cases are (n1-1) : (n2-1).
```

```
  PATTERN 18 — TWO WAVELENGTHS, TWO STOPPING POTENTIALS           4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "With 3000 A the stopping potential is 1.85 V; with 4000 A
            it is 0.82 V. Find the work function (or h)."
  METHOD    1. Write Einstein's equation twice:
                 E1 = W0 + eV1        E2 = W0 + eV2
            2. To find W0: use either equation directly (and check with
               the other -- they must agree).
            3. To find h: SUBTRACT to eliminate W0:
                 h = e(V1 - V2) / [ c (1/lambda1 - 1/lambda2) ]
  TRAP      Not checking the second equation. If the two values of W0
            disagree, you made an arithmetic slip.
```

```
  PATTERN 19 — EFFECT OF CHANGING INTENSITY / DISTANCE            2 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "If the intensity is doubled ..." / "If the source is moved
            to twice the distance ..."
  METHOD    1. Intensity changes ONLY the saturation current, in direct
               proportion.
            2. Kmax and V0 do NOT change at all.
            3. If the DISTANCE is involved, intensity ~ 1/r^2, so
               doubling r makes the current 1/4.
  TRAP      Changing V0. It never changes when only the intensity
            changes.
```

```
  PATTERN 20 — DESCRIBE THE PHOTOCELL                             4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Describe the construction and working of a photocell.
            State its uses."
  METHOD    1. Diagram: evacuated glass bulb, semi-cylindrical cathode
               coated with caesium (LOW work function), thin axial rod
               anode kept POSITIVE, micro-ammeter and battery.
            2. Working: light -> photoelectrons -> collected by the
               positive anode -> current PROPORTIONAL to intensity.
            3. Uses: cinema sound track, burglar and fire alarms,
               automatic doors and street lights, exposure meters,
               counting devices, television.
  TRAP      Not saying WHY caesium is used (its work function is only
            2.14 eV, so ordinary visible light works).
```

```
  PATTERN 21 — STATE / DERIVE de BROGLIE'S RELATION               2-4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "State de Broglie's hypothesis" / "Derive lambda = h/p."
  METHOD    1. State the hypothesis: nature is symmetrical, so every
               moving material particle has a matter wave.
            2. For a photon: E = h nu  and  E = m c^2 .
            3. Equate: h nu = m c^2 , so m c = h nu / c = h / lambda .
            4. m c is the momentum p, so p = h/lambda .
            5. Generalise to any particle: lambda = h/p = h/(mv).
  TRAP      Stopping at the photon. The final step -- "de Broglie
            proposed this holds for ALL particles" -- is the whole
            point of the derivation.
```

```
  PATTERN 22 — de BROGLIE WAVELENGTH FROM MASS AND VELOCITY       2 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Find the de Broglie wavelength of a 150 g ball moving at
            30 m/s" / "of an electron moving at 10^6 m/s."
  METHOD    lambda = h / (m v) , with h = 6.63 x 10^-34.
            Then comment on the order of magnitude.
  TRAP      Leaving the mass in grams. Convert to kilograms first.
```

```
  PATTERN 23 — de BROGLIE WAVELENGTH FROM KINETIC ENERGY          2-4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Find the wavelength of an electron of kinetic energy
            120 eV / 1 keV."
  METHOD    lambda = h / sqrt(2 m K).
            SHORTCUT for an electron with K in eV:
                 lambda = 12.27 / sqrt( K in eV )  angstrom
  TRAP      Using the 12.27 shortcut for a PROTON or an ALPHA particle.
            Those constants are 0.286 and 0.101 respectively.
```

```
  PATTERN 24 — de BROGLIE WAVELENGTH OF AN ACCELERATED PARTICLE   4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "An electron is accelerated through V volts. Find its
            wavelength" / "Derive lambda = 12.27/sqrt(V)."
  METHOD    1. K = eV  (gained from the accelerating field).
            2. p = sqrt(2 m e V).
            3. lambda = h / sqrt(2 m e V).
            4. Substitute h, m, e -> lambda = 12.27/sqrt(V) angstrom.
            Reverse direction: given lambda, V = (12.27/lambda)^2 .
  TRAP      Writing the answer in metres but labelling it angstrom.
            12.27/sqrt(V) is in ANGSTROM; divide by 10^10 for metres.
```

```
  PATTERN 25 — de BROGLIE WAVELENGTH AT A TEMPERATURE             2-4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Find the wavelength of a thermal neutron / a gas molecule
            at 300 K."
  METHOD    1. K = (3/2) k T  with k = 1.38 x 10^-23 J/K.
            2. lambda = h / sqrt(2 m K) = h / sqrt(3 m k T).
  TRAP      Using T in Celsius. It must be in KELVIN.
```

```
  PATTERN 26 — COMPARE TWO PARTICLES' WAVELENGTHS                 2 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "An electron and a proton have the same ... Which has the
            longer wavelength?"
  METHOD    Read WHAT is the same, then apply:
              same MOMENTUM          ->  lambda EQUAL
              same SPEED             ->  lambda ~ 1/m
              same KINETIC ENERGY    ->  lambda ~ 1/sqrt(m)
              same ACCELERATING V    ->  lambda ~ 1/sqrt(m q)
            The lighter particle always wins except in the
            same-momentum case, where it is a tie.
  TRAP      Not reading which quantity is held constant. Three different
            answers come from three different conditions.
```

```
  PATTERN 27 — WHY IS THE WAVE NATURE OF BIG OBJECTS UNSEEN?      2 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Why do we not observe the wave nature of a moving cricket
            ball / bullet / car?"
  METHOD    1. lambda = h/(mv) and h = 6.63 x 10^-34 is tiny while m
               is comparatively huge, so lambda is about 10^-34 m.
            2. Diffraction needs an obstacle COMPARABLE to the
               wavelength; no such obstacle exists (even a nucleus is
               10^-15 m).
            3. Conclude: the wave nature is UNDETECTABLE, not absent.
  TRAP      Saying "big objects have no wave nature". They do. It is
            simply too small to measure.
```

```
  PATTERN 28 — DESCRIBE THE DAVISSON-GERMER EXPERIMENT            4 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Describe the Davisson-Germer experiment" / "How was the
            wave nature of the electron confirmed?"
  METHOD    1. Diagram: filament, accelerating anode (variable V),
               collimator, NICKEL crystal, movable detector on a
               circular arc.
            2. Observation: a sharp intensity peak at phi = 50 degrees
               when V = 54 volts.
            3. de Broglie prediction: 12.27/sqrt(54) = 1.67 A.
            4. Measured from Bragg's law with d = 0.91 A and
               theta = (180-50)/2 = 65 deg :
                 lambda = 2 d sin theta = 1.65 A.
            5. CONCLUSION: the agreement confirms the wave nature of
               the electron.
  TRAP      Swapping 54 and 50, or confusing the SCATTERING angle
            (50 deg) with the BRAGG glancing angle (65 deg).
```

```
  PATTERN 29 — HEISENBERG'S UNCERTAINTY PRINCIPLE                 2 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "State the uncertainty principle" / "Find the minimum
            uncertainty in momentum if the position is known to ..."
  METHOD    1. State it: position and momentum cannot both be known
               exactly at the same instant.
            2. (delta x)(delta p) >= h/(4 pi).
            3. For a numerical: delta p = h / (4 pi delta x).
            4. If asked WHY: a wave of one exact wavelength (one exact
               momentum) is spread over all space, so its position is
               completely undetermined.
  TRAP      Using h/2pi. The standard board form is h/(4 pi).
```

```
  PATTERN 30 — THE FULL LONG-ANSWER ESSAY                         8 marks
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  TRIGGER   "Describe the photoelectric effect experiment, state the
            results and explain them using Einstein's equation."
  METHOD    Budget the eight marks:
              2  -  labelled experimental diagram + purpose of parts
              3  -  the four results WITH their four graphs
              1  -  the photon postulate
              1  -  derivation of h nu = W0 + Kmax
              1  -  explanation of each result from that equation
  TRAP      Spending all the time on the diagram and running out of
            space for the graphs. The graphs carry more marks than the
            circuit does.
```

# GUARANTEED-MARKS PLAN FOR THIS CHAPTER

```
  STEP 1   MEMORISE THESE FOUR LINES. They alone are worth 4-6 marks.
  -------------------------------------------------------------------
           h nu  =  W0 + Kmax          Kmax = e V0 = h(nu - nu0)
           E(eV) = 12400 / lambda(A)   lambda = 12.27/sqrt(V) angstrom

  STEP 2   LEARN THE FIVE DEFINITIONS WORD-PERFECT (Pattern 1).
  -------------------------------------------------------------------
           work function, threshold frequency, threshold wavelength,
           stopping potential, saturation current.
           Any one of these is a free 2-mark question.

  STEP 3   PRACTISE DRAWING THE FOUR GRAPHS UNTIL THEY ARE AUTOMATIC.
  -------------------------------------------------------------------
           current vs intensity ; current vs potential ;
           V0 vs frequency ; Kmax vs frequency.
           Label BOTH axes and mark nu0 , -V0 and the saturation level.

  STEP 4   WRITE OUT PATTERNS 4, 7, 8 AND 13 IN FULL, ONCE, BY HAND.
  -------------------------------------------------------------------
           laws of emission / failure of wave theory / Einstein's
           equation / Davisson-Germer. One of these WILL be your
           4-mark question.

  STEP 5   DO TEN NUMERICALS: five photoelectric, five de Broglie.
  -------------------------------------------------------------------
           Always: formula first, then substitution, then answer with
           the UNIT. Sanity-check the order of magnitude against the
           table in 02-formulae.md section 10.

  -------------------------------------------------------------------
  DO THIS AND YOU WILL SCORE 6 TO 10 MARKS FROM A CHAPTER THAT TAKES
  ONLY TWO EVENINGS TO LEARN. There is no cheaper chapter in the
  Physics syllabus.
  -------------------------------------------------------------------
```

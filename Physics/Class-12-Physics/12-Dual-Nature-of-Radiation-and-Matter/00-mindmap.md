## THE 60-SECOND VERSION

```
  Old idea:   light = wave        electron = particle

  This chapter destroys both halves of that sentence.

  1. Shine light on a metal. Electrons jump out. But they only jump out if the
     light's FREQUENCY is high enough. A dim blue lamp works. A blinding red
     lamp does nothing. A wave could never behave like that.

  2. Einstein's fix:  light travels as tiny packets called PHOTONS, each
     carrying energy  E = h nu.  One photon hits one electron. All or nothing.

           h nu   =   W0    +    Kmax
          (packet)  (exit    (leftover energy
                     fee)     of the electron)

  3. de Broglie then asked the reverse question: if a wave can act like a
     particle, can a particle act like a wave? Yes.

           lambda  =  h / p  =  h / (m v)

  4. Davisson and Germer fired electrons at a nickel crystal and got a
     DIFFRACTION pattern -- the signature of a wave. Proof.
```

Everything in the chapter is a comment on those four boxes.

## THE TRAP THAT COSTS THE MOST MARKS

```
  INTENSITY  ->  HOW MANY photons per second  ->  controls the NUMBER of
                 electrons  ->  controls the CURRENT.

  FREQUENCY  ->  HOW MUCH energy per photon   ->  controls the ENERGY of
                 each electron  ->  controls Kmax and V0.

  They NEVER swap jobs.
  Brighter light does NOT give faster electrons.
  Higher frequency does NOT give more current.
```

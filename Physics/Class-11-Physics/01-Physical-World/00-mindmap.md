## THE 60-SECOND VERSION

```
  1.  Physics = the study of the basic laws of nature.
  2.  It works over a HUGE range:
         lengths  10^-14 m  to  10^26 m
         masses   10^-30 kg to  10^55 kg
         times    10^-22 s  to  10^18 s
  3.  Big things  -> MACROSCOPIC domain -> classical physics.
      Tiny things -> MICROSCOPIC domain -> quantum mechanics.
  4.  There are exactly FOUR forces in nature:
         gravitational, electromagnetic, strong nuclear, weak nuclear.
      Strength order:  gravity < weak < electromagnetic < strong.
  5.  Some quantities NEVER change in an isolated system:
         energy, linear momentum, angular momentum, charge, mass-energy.
      These are the CONSERVATION LAWS. They come from SYMMETRIES of nature.
  6.  Physics and technology feed each other both ways.
```

That is the entire chapter. Genuinely.

**Memory hook for the strength order (weakest to strongest):**

```
        G   <   W   <   EM   <   S
      "Go     With    Every    Second"

  Gravity is the WEAKEST but you feel it most, because it is always
  attractive and never cancels out. Electric forces are far stronger
  but + and - cancel each other, so you never notice them.
```

That is exactly how long this chapter deserves. Here is the plan:

```
  MINUTE 0-10   Copy the FOUR FORCES table above, by hand, twice.
                Say out loud: "gravity, weak, electromagnetic, strong"
                and "gravity weakest, strong strongest".
```
